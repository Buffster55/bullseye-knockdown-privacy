Privacy Policy

Bullseye Knockdown

Last updated: November 11, 2026

At a glance: Bullseye Knockdown keeps ordinary game progress and local rankings on your device. When you are online and play an eligible mode, the game uses Meta Horizon Platform services for an entitlement check and worldwide leaderboards. Worldwide entries are displayed as rank and score only, without usernames or profile photos. The game has no independent leaderboard server, advertising, or in-app purchases.

1. Who operates the game

Bullseye Knockdown is developed and operated by Robert Vandal, sole proprietor operating as BuffstersQuest, Québec, Canada. This policy describes the game's handling of information on Meta Quest devices. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to buffstersquest-support.skating711@passfwd.com.

2. Information the game uses

  • Local gameplay data: scores, rankings, settings, and saved progress are stored on your headset so the game can work offline. Those local records do not automatically upload later when you reconnect.
  • Meta platform identity and entitlement: while using Meta platform features, the game uses the Meta account's platform User ID as needed for authentication, entitlement, and worldwide leaderboard operations. The game does not write this platform User ID into its local save file.
  • Worldwide leaderboard scores: an eligible completed Challenge result or Coin Visit can be sent through Meta's leaderboard service. Four boards distinguish Challenge versus Coin Visit and one-handed versus two-handed play. Meta associates entries with the platform account so rankings can operate. The game retrieves leaderboard rankings and scores, including your own placement.
  • VR input: headset and controller pose/input information is processed to run the game. Bullseye Knockdown does not independently record or transmit microphone audio, camera images, or videos.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, your email address and the information you provide are used to respond to your inquiry.

The game does not display or collect Meta usernames or profile photos for its worldwide ranking screen, and does not use friends lists, social invitations, in-app purchases, advertising trackers, or a separate analytics service. Meta itself may process additional account, device, and service data under its own policies.

3. How information is used

Local information is used to continue your game and provide offline records. Meta platform information is used to verify access and provide the game's four worldwide leaderboards. A completed eligible online result is submitted according to the control/reload method locked for that play session. If offline, local play remains available; the game does not queue old results to upload after reconnecting. Support information is used only for communication and handling a request.

4. Sharing and service providers

Worldwide score submissions and leaderboard reads are handled by Meta Horizon Platform. Meta operates the platform service and may process account-linked leaderboard data under its own terms and privacy policy. The game's leaderboard display shows ranks and scores without usernames or profile pictures. BuffstersQuest does not operate its own score collection server and does not sell player information. Email messages sent to our support address are processed by the email service involved in delivering them.

For details on Meta's handling of platform information, see Meta's privacy policy.

5. Children and age group

Bullseye Knockdown is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, or the applicable minimum age in the user's region. It is not intended for younger children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children below the applicable age threshold. If you believe information from an underage child has been provided to us, please contact the support address above.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • On-device data: game saves and local rankings remain on the device until removed by the user, an app-data reset, or uninstall (subject to the headset's own backup or restore settings).
  • Meta leaderboard data: Meta hosts worldwide entries. Its retention, account controls, and platform deletion mechanisms apply. To request assistance with an entry, contact us with the relevant game and board details; we may need to direct you to Meta's available account controls or support processes.
  • Support correspondence: emails may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain relevant records, and satisfy legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed.

7. Your choices and rights

You can play with local records when offline, manage or clear local app data through your device, and choose whether to send a support email. Meta account settings govern Meta's platform-held information. You may contact BuffstersQuest at buffstersquest-support.skating711@passfwd.com to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information that we control. We will respond to requests under applicable law and explain when an action must be handled by Meta.

8. Security

We minimize the information handled by the game, do not maintain a separate account database, and use the Meta platform services provided for entitlement and worldwide score operations. No internet-connected service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy if the game or its data practices change. We will update the date above and publish the revised policy at this same public address. Material changes should be reviewed before a new game version is released.
